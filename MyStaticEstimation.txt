MyStaticEstimation.txt: improved static estimation (evaluation.py)
 
1. Features (all scored from White's point of view, White minus Black)
   - Piece difference: the handout's main idea. Pieces win the game.
   - Mills: completed three-in-a-row lines. Mill pieces cannot be captured.
   - Threats: lines with two own pieces and one empty spot (a mill next move).
   - Mobility (midgame/endgame): number of legal moves for each side.
 
2. Why these features
   - The opening estimate only sees piece difference, so most placements tie at
     0 and search picks the first empty spot (a0). Mills and threats tell
     search which placements actually build something.
   - The handout midgame estimate only counts Black's moves. Counting both sides
     rewards keeping White free as well as boxing Black in.
   - Blocked players lose, so mobility is the cheapest warning of that.
 
3. How they are combined (weighted sum, weights ordered by importance)
   improved_opening = 10*pieces + 100*mills + 40*threats
   improved_game    = 1000*pieces + 150*mills + 60*threats + 20*mobility
   Terminal positions are unchanged: Black <= 2 pieces or Black blocked = +10000,
   White <= 2 pieces = -10000. In the midgame one piece is worth more than any
   combination of the other features, as in the handout.
 
4. Why it should be better
   It keeps the handout's winning logic and adds positional knowledge, so
   it can separate moves the original scores as equal. It costs one extra
   mobility call per leaf, which is acceptable at tournament depths.
 
Positions where the improved evaluation picks a different move
(White to move, opening, depth 2 for both evaluators; run with
MiniMaxOpening.py and MiniMaxOpeningImproved.py)
 
Position A: xxxxxWxxxxxxxxxBxxWxxBx   (W on 5 and 18, B on 15 and 21)
   Original: WxxxxWxxxxxxxxxBxxWxxBx  estimate 0   (places at a0)
   Improved: xxxxxWxxxxxxxxxBxxWWxBx  estimate 40  (places at 19)
   Why: every placement ties for the original, so it takes the first empty
   spot, which builds nothing. Placing at 19 makes two threats at once:
   5-12-19 needs 12 and 17-18-19 needs 17. Black can block only one, so White
   keeps a threat after Black's reply (estimate +40).
 
Position B: WxxxxxxxWxWBxxxxxxxBWBB   (W on 0, 8, 10, 20; B on 11, 19, 21, 22)
   Original: WxxxxxxxWWWxxxxxxxxBWBB  estimate 1   (mill 8-9-10, removes B at 11)
   Improved: WxxxxxxxWWWBxxxxxxxxWBB  estimate 130 (mill 8-9-10, removes B at 19)
   Why: both play 9 and close the same mill, but differ on the capture.
   The original takes the first removable piece (11). The improved one removes
   19, which breaks Black's threat 19-22 on line 16-19-22 (Black has 0 threats
   afterwards instead of 1) and keeps White's lead.
