MORRIS TOURNAMENT CHEAT SHEET
=============================

BEFORE THE GAME
---------------
1. Open a terminal and go to the project folder:
       cd ~/Desktop/morris-ai-project
       source venv/bin/activate
2. Teams chat with the other team is open. One person on your team is the
   "communicator" and is the only one who posts boards.
3. Know your color. White always moves first.
   - Game 1: Team A is White, Team B is Black.
   - Game 2: swap.

MOVE NUMBERS
------------
White plays odd moves:  1, 3, 5, 7, ...
Black plays even moves: 2, 4, 6, 8, ...
Moves 1-18 are placements. Move 19 and later are piece movements.
Always pass the correct move number to the agent.


IF YOU ARE WHITE (you post first)
=================================
Step 1. Start the game from the empty board:

    echo xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx > in.txt
    python TournamentAgent.py in.txt out.txt W 1

Step 2. The agent prints two lines at the end, for example:

    Move 1 - White
    Wxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx

Post exactly those two lines in the Teams chat. The game has started.

Step 3. Wait for Black to post "Move 2 - Black" and a board.

Step 4. Check Black's move. Previous board = the board YOU posted at move 1:

    python check_move.py <your_last_board> <board_black_posted> 2

Step 5. Save Black's board and get your next move (move 3):

    echo <board_black_posted> > in.txt
    python TournamentAgent.py in.txt out.txt W 3

Step 6. Post the two printed lines. Repeat with W 5, W 7, W 9, ...


IF YOU ARE BLACK (you wait first)
=================================
Step 1. Do nothing until White posts "Move 1 - White" and a board.

Step 2. Check White's first move (previous board is the empty board):

    python check_move.py xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx <board_white_posted> 1

Step 3. Save White's board and get your reply (move 2):

    echo <board_white_posted> > in.txt
    python TournamentAgent.py in.txt out.txt B 2

Step 4. Post the two printed lines:

    Move 2 - Black
    <23-character board>

Step 5. Wait for White's next post, check it, and repeat with B 4, B 6, B 8, ...
        Check command each time (previous board = the board YOU posted last):

    python check_move.py <your_last_board> <board_white_posted> 3


EVERY TURN (same loop for both colors)
======================================
1. Opponent posts a board.
2. Check it:     python check_move.py <your_last_board> <their_board> <their_move_number>
3. Save it:      echo <their_board> > in.txt
4. Run agent:    python TournamentAgent.py in.txt out.txt <W or B> <your_move_number>
5. Post the two printed lines ("Move N - Color" and the board).

You have 4 MINUTES from their post. The agent stops thinking at 120 seconds.


IF THEIR MOVE IS ILLEGAL
------------------------
check_move.py prints ILLEGAL and shows which spots changed.
You may claim the game, but only BEFORE you post your own reply.
Tell them in the chat first. If there is a disagreement, save the chat
and contact the instructor.


OTHER THINGS TO WATCH
---------------------
- Boards must be exactly 23 characters (only W, B, x). Double-check the paste.
- DRAW: the same board with the same player to move 3 times. The agent does
  not detect this. Watch the chat and point it out.
- TIMEOUT: if the opponent does not post within 4 minutes, you may claim the
  game (or choose to restart; you never have to).
- WIN: the opponent is down to 2 pieces, or has no legal move.
- Keep your laptop plugged in and close heavy apps.


AFTER BOTH GAMES
----------------
One team adds one line to the shared results document, exactly:

    MATCH|T01|T07|A|B|2026-10-15

Meaning:  MATCH|TeamA|TeamB|Game1|Game2|date
Codes:    A = Team A won, B = Team B won, D = draw
Game 1 is first, game 2 second. Use team IDs (T01...), not team names.
Save the Teams conversation as the official record.


PRACTICE FIRST (recommended)
----------------------------
Play yourself against the agent:
    python practice.py B 20     (agent is Black, you type White's boards)
    python practice.py W 20     (agent is White)
Watch the agent play itself:
    python selfplay.py
